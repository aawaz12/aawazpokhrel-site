When I look at an emerging technology, I try to separate a few questions that
often get blurred together:

- **Does it work?** What's the strength of the supporting evidence?
- **Is it differentiated?** Meaningfully, or only on paper?
- **What could break it?** What would prevent successful development?
- **Does it matter?** Is the problem important enough to create lasting value?
